Number Task Study

Thank you for helping with this class research project for MIE 657 (Human Factors) at UMass Amherst.

You will see a series of short numbers, one at a time, and type each one back. It takes about 5 minutes.

Taking part is voluntary, and you can stop at any time by closing this page. No names or contact details are collected. Your answers are stored anonymously and used only for this class project.

Instructions

  1. A number will appear on the screen.
  2. Look at it for as long as you need, then press Next.
  3. The number will disappear. Type the number you just saw, then press Submit.
  4. This repeats for each number.

Please don't write anything down or use your phone during the task.

One last question

Please try to remember all of the numbers you saw during the task, not just the last one.

Type each number next to the position where you saw it. If you only remember part of a number, type the digits you remember. Leave a box empty if you don't remember that number at all.

Two quick questions

Before that last question, did you expect to be asked about the earlier numbers?
Do you have any pilot or air traffic control experience?

Thank you!

Saving your responses…

The last question was a surprise on purpose. This study looks at how much people remember of information they thought they were finished with, like a radio frequency a pilot has already dialed in. Telling you ahead of time would have changed how you approached the task.

Please don't tell anyone else who might take this study about the surprise question.
